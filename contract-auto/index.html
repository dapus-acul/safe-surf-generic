Privacy Policy / Politică de Confidențialitate

Contract Auto does not represent a government entity. It is a private tool for filling in public forms. It is not issued by, endorsed by, or connected with any public authority, and it does not provide official services. Sources for the public information it shows: dgpci.mai.gov.ro (vehicle registration), mdlpa.ro (the ITL form templates), legislatie.just.ro (legal texts) and your local tax office. Requirements vary by locality and change over time — always check at the source.

ENGLISH

1. Overview

Contract Auto is an Android application that generates vehicle sale contracts (Vânzare-Cumpărare) as PDF files. This policy explains how we handle any information entered into the app.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No data is sent to any server or third party
  • No analytics or tracking libraries are used
  • No account or registration is required
  • No data is stored outside your device

3. Data You Enter

The app requires you to enter personal details (names, addresses, CNP/national ID numbers) to generate the contract PDF. This data:

  • Is used only to generate the PDF document on your device
  • Is never transmitted over the internet
  • Is not stored by the app after the PDF is generated
  • Remains entirely under your control — the PDF is saved to your device only

4. PDF Files

Generated PDF contracts are saved locally on your device (in your Downloads folder or a location you choose). You are responsible for managing and deleting these files.

5. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or cloud services.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adults (18+) for legal/business use. It is not directed at children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For any questions: danpop1907@gmail.com
