Privacy Policy — safeSurf

Android app, package com.safesurfbrowse.app, by Acul Digital.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The short version. safeSurf has no servers. Nothing you do is uploaded to us, because there is nowhere for it to go. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the email report, and it is sent directly from the email account you configure to the person you choose — it does not pass through us. We cannot read it, and we have no way of knowing you are using the app at all.

What leaves your device

One thing: the email report, and only if you have given consent in the app. It goes from the email account you configured, directly to the recipient you chose, over your email provider's normal connection. Acul Digital operates no server in that path and never receives a copy.

The report contains:

  • whether the filter is switched on;
  • how many times it acted that day;
  • if, and only if, you switched on detailed reporting: the terms that triggered a block, with the time and the app.

The report never contains a browsing history, because safeSurf does not keep one. It only ever describes what was blocked.

Your consent, and withdrawing it

No email is sent until you tick the consent box during setup. You can stop all reporting at any time in the app, and you can switch detailed reporting off separately while keeping the daily summary. Turning detailed reporting off also stops anything being recorded, so there is no history left behind to be found later.

If safeSurf is used on a child's phone

Where a parent or guardian sets safeSurf up on a child's device, the parent is responsible for that decision and for the consent given during setup. The app is visible on the device, its icon cannot be hidden, and it shows a permanent notification while protection is running. safeSurf is not designed to monitor anyone secretly and must not be used to do so.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service — reads text shown on screen so explicit content can be detected and blocked. The text is examined on the device and is never stored or transmitted.
  • VPN — runs a local DNS filter. No traffic is tunnelled anywhere: only name lookups enter the app, so pages, messages and video are never seen by it. There is no remote VPN server.
  • Notifications — the permanent notice shown while protection is active.
  • Run at startup — so protection resumes after a restart.

Payments

The subscription is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details, and we receive no personal information about you from Google beyond anonymous sales totals. Manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play app.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no advertising identifier.
  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, for any purpose.
  • No collection of location, contacts, photos, files or call history.

Your rights

Because all data stays on your device, you control it directly: clear the app's data or uninstall it and nothing remains. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. Under the GDPR you retain your rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection — in practice they are exercised on the device itself.

Children's data

safeSurf is not directed at children for the purpose of collecting their data, and it collects none for us. Where it is installed on a child's phone by a parent, the data described above stays on that phone, except for the report sent to the parent's own chosen address.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
